## Store facts
- **Name:** Yale Bulldog Blue by Campus Customs. It's an officially licensed Yale merchandise retailer.
- **Location:** 57 Broadway, New Haven, CT 06511. The store is open 7 days a week.
- **History:** established in 1975. It's described as the oldest official Yale merchandise retailer in New Haven, and the owner is Barry Cobden.
- **Production:** most screen printing and embroidery happen on site, in the former York Square Cinema building, which closed in 2005.
- **Services:** screen printing, embroidery, digital printing, and promotional items for teams, clubs, and groups.
- **Contact:** orderdept@campuscustoms.com and (475) 301-4205. The site also offers help by chat.
- **Shipping:** ships internationally to 25+ countries with local currency options.

## Return policy (paraphrased)
- Items can be returned within 30 days of shipping. They must be unworn and unused, with the original tags attached.
- Custom-made items, such as custom alumni products, are final sale.
- To start a return, email your tracking number to the order department. Use insured, trackable shipping.
- The store pays return shipping when the mistake was theirs. Otherwise the customer pays it.
- Refunds take 2 to 10 business days after the return arrives, and original shipping charges aren't refunded.

## Catalogue and audience on the real site
- **Categories:** Clothing, Accessories, Home, Collections, Residential Colleges, Sports, and Graduate Schools.
- **Audience:** students, alumni, and families, with relative collections such as Mom, Dad, and Aunt, plus class-year collections.
- **Our local DB only has tops:** hoodies, crewnecks, T-shirts, quarter-zips, fleece, and jackets. The agent must not claim to stock anything that isn't in `campus_customs.db`.

## Style notes
- **Real site:** navy and white, with a collegiate, community-focused, spirited-but-casual tone built around "Bulldog pride" messaging. It uses hero banners and product grids.
- **Our build:** keeps that layout and tone, restyled black and pink per the project instructions, with a serif display font for a collegiate feel.
